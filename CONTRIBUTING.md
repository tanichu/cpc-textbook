# Comments and corrections

> **Draft policy.** The terms below are provisional and may change before the first public
> draft is released.

Thank you for reading. This book is being written in the open, and comments genuinely help.

## What is welcome

- Typographical errors.
- Errors in the mathematics — wrong indices, missing conditions, broken derivations.
- Suggestions about content, framing, or what is missing.
- Pointers to literature that should be cited.

Comments may be written in **English or Japanese**. どちらでも構いません。

## How to comment

Open an issue: <https://github.com/tanichu/cpc-textbook/issues>

Please say which chapter and, if you can, which equation or paragraph you mean.

Additional channels — threaded discussion attached to each chapter, inline annotation, and
an anonymous form — are under consideration and are not yet available.

## Terms

- The author reserves the right to reflect any comment or suggestion in the eventual
  published book. Making a comment does not create a claim to compensation or to
  co-authorship.
- Contributors will be named in the acknowledgements of the published book.

## Please do not

- Cite, quote, or redistribute the current contents. Nothing here has been verified by the
  author, and everything is subject to change.
